Wide screen for SV gain with a standard PGS method: pre-specification

Fixed on 2026-10-22 at about 23:40 PDT, before any screen run was submitted and before any screen trait's held-out
value existed. Amendments go below with their time, and only before the analysis runs.

Question
- For which traits do imputed SVs raise the held-out accuracy of LDpred2-auto?

Method
- LDpred2-auto alone (aou_fastpgs.py --methods ldpred2_auto, run by aou_fastpgs_screen.sh as each screen run's
  after-script). lassosum2 and C+T are dropped (user, 2026-10-22).
- Each trait is fitted twice on the same training people: on the snv_indel records and on every kept record
  (with_sv). Both fits share the GWAS pass, the 3 cM band and the settings of the 11-trait battery. Scores go to the
  run's held-out people.
- A screen run holds several traits. Its traits share one read of the codes, the restore and the steps before the
  fit. The fits and scores of each trait are its own.

Statistic and test (fastpgs_meta.py --screen ldpred2_auto --exclude <long-read manifest>)
- Long-read panel members (the research ids of v9/wgs/long_read/manifest.tsv in the CDR) are dropped from every
  trait's held-out people first, as in SV_GAIN_PRESPEC.txt. Only their count is printed.
- Per trait: gain = with_sv partial r^2 less snv_indel partial r^2, given the trait's covariates, on its held-out
  people. SE = the SD of the gain over 2,000 family bootstrap resamples drawn once over the union of all traits'
  held-out people. Test: one-sided, H1 gain > 0, p = 1 - Phi(gain / SE).
- Family: every trait below that passes the power threshold. Methods never multiply the family: there is one method.
- Multiplicity: Benjamini-Hochberg over the family. A trait with q < 0.05 is a screen hit.
- Reported for every trait in the family: both arms' partial r^2, the gain with its bootstrap 95% interval, the gain
  relative to the snv_indel arm, the one-sided p and the q. Hits and non-hits are both reported.

Power threshold and the count rule (applied in code before any value is printed)
- A disease enters with at least 100 held-out cases and 100 held-out controls. A quantitative trait enters with at
  least 1,000 held-out people.
- Traits below the threshold are named and nothing else about them is printed. This drops every trait with fewer
  than 40 cases before anyone looks at it.
- No count below 40 is printed or read (shown as <40). No summary of a group under 20 people is read.

Traits in the family
- The 11 traits of the earlier battery (LDpred2-auto results already read, so their inclusion is fixed now and
  does not depend on them): atrial_fibrillation (aou-m1p-a), coronary_artery_disease (aou-m1p-c), gout (aou-m1p-g),
  hypertension (aou-m1p-h), chronic_kidney_disease (aou-m1p-k), hypothyroidism (aou-m1p-o), asthma (aou-m1p-s),
  type2_diabetes (aou-m1p-t), hyperlipidemia (aou-m1p-y), egfr_ckd_epi_2021 (aou-we6), ldl_cholesterol (aou-wl5).
- Screen run aou-scr-d1: breast_cancer, copd, depression, cataract, psoriasis, prostate_cancer, alzheimers_disease.
- Screen run aou-scr-q1: heart_rate, mean_corpuscular_volume, platelet_count, white_blood_cell_count,
  total_bilirubin.
- New definitions (sv_pgs/all_of_us.py, codes checked on tx.fhir.org and units from aggregate LOINC unit counts) may
  be added in further screen runs by amendment, only before the analysis runs and only while no screen run's held-out
  value has been read.
- Not in the family: the eight traits of family Q8 (height, BMI, SBP, DBP, HbA1c, HDL, TG, TC). They are blind until
  Q8's own test (SV_GAIN_PRESPEC.txt amendment 3), and their fast baselines are Q8's secondary.

Blinding
- Until the analysis runs, nobody reads a screen run's fastpgs.jsonl, the JSON lines of its after-task log, or its
  fastpgs/*.npz. Runs are watched by job state, markers and the after-task log's timestamped progress lines only.

When
- Once, when every screen run listed above has finished or failed, or at 2026-10-23 14:00 PDT, whichever is first.
  A run that has not finished by then is listed as missing, and its traits leave the family.

Follow-up
- A screen hit is a candidate for replication in the new 50k (SV_GAIN_PRESPEC.txt and sv_gain_replication.py), with
  its own pre-specification before any of its new values is computed.
