1SV effects can have a sign

Polygenic scores mostly leave out structural variants, or credit them through tagging SNVs.

  • SVs are enriched for heritability in 31 of 105 UK Biobank traits, median 14.8× (MiXeR-SV, preprint).
  • An SV’s ALT allele is a physical event, so its effect can have a biological sign.

2One model for every variant

Every record, from a single-base indel to a large deletion, enters one model. No length bins.

y = Cα + Σj Dj βj + ε
βj ~ ∫ N(0, uj s) gc(s) ds
log uj = log(κj2 Var Dj)reliability
+ h(log Var Gj)frequency
+ dj⊤θannotations
  • The mixing density gc is learned per class: continuous, nonparametric, roughness-penalized. Gaussian and BayesR-style priors are special cases.
  • Annotations are smooths with learned smoothness: log length, distance to TSS, allele frequency.
  • A tandem-repeat locus is one column: its signed total length.
  • Imputed SVs act like confident draws, not calibrated means: at MAF 1–5%, κ 0.80 vs √r² 0.83 semi-real. Reliability sets each variant’s prior scale.

3Fit it; refuse what did not converge

Empirical Bayes: hyperparameters maximize the approximate evidence at a fixed point of the approximate posterior.

  • Newton steps on the total curvature, with a trust region.
  • A fit is scored only if converged: remaining gain ≤ 1/(2K) nats. Others are quarantined.
