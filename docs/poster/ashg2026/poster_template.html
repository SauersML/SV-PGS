1Structural variant effects can have a sign

Polygenic scores mostly leave out SVs (≥ 50 bp deletions, insertions, duplications) and tandem repeats, or credit them through tagging SNVs.

  • SVs are enriched for heritability in 31 of 105 UK Biobank traits, median 14.8× (MiXeR-SV; Nguyen et al. 2026, preprint).
  • They are measured badly: common SVs impute at median r² 0.78 from a long-read panel (Bai et al. 2026).
  • An SV’s ALT allele is a physical event, so its effect can carry a biological sign. A SNV’s REF/ALT orientation carries none.

A symmetric prior puts equal mass on both signs here. A location-scale extension of the mixing law is specified and waits on the inference route.

2One generative model for every variant

Every record enters one model, from a single-base indel to a large deletion. No length bins, no separate SV model.

Dj: stored dosage, standardized in the training fold · Gj: true genotype · κj: calibration slope of D on G · C: sex, age, PCs, imputation batch, projected out exactly first
  • The mixing density gc is learned per class, continuous and nonparametric, with no point mass at zero. A third-order roughness penalty with a learned weight; its λ → ∞ limit is the log-normal. Gaussian, BayesR-style grids and heavy tails are special cases.
  • Continuous annotations are smooths with learned smoothness (log length, distance to the TSS, allele frequency); discrete ones (variant class, gene and exon overlap) stay discrete. A smooth’s linear term gets a learned ridge of its own (the double penalty; Marra & Wood 2011).
  • A tandem-repeat locus is one column, its signed total length Σa Δlena Dia, so the score does not depend on which allele the reference carries.
  • Imputed SVs behave like confident draws, not calibrated means. At MAF 1–5%, κ was 0.80 against √r² 0.83, and Var(DS)/Var(G) 0.89 against r² 0.69 semi-real. Reliability enters each variant’s prior scale through κ, never the LD matrix.

3Fitting it, and refusing what did not converge

Empirical Bayes: the prior’s hyperparameters maximize the approximate evidence at a fixed point of the approximate posterior.

  • Two fixed points: expectation propagation, and mean-field coordinate ascent (the store route’s default). Which one stays is decided on held-out accuracy.
  • Hyper steps are Newton steps on the total curvature with a trust region. The evidence is the Laplace value plus leading Tierney–Kadane terms, the largest replaced by exact line integrals.
  • A fit is scored only if it converged: remaining gain ≤ 1/(2K) nats for K posterior draws. A model that fails is quarantined; its trait leaves the report and the others stay. This is a numerical criterion, not a bound on posterior error.
A flat prior on a smooth’s linear term has no maximum

For a group of signal-free variants the evidence keeps rising as their prior variance shrinks, so empirical Bayes walked one linear coefficient to 5 × 10⁴. A learned ridge on each smooth’s null space restores a maximum. On a 240-person annotated benchmark machinery:

4Public benchmarks no method controls

  • bench-real real: MAGE lymphoblastoid expression in 730 people from 26 populations, with 1kGP high-coverage SNV, indel and SV calls in TSS ± 1 Mb windows. Family-grouped 5-fold and leave-one-superpopulation-out splits; chromosome-jackknife SEs.
  • bench-sim: 50,000 mosaic genomes copied from 1kGP founder haplotypes, with SVs and tandem repeats masked and re-imputed by Beagle from a disjoint panel. A pre-registered, deliberately misspecified truth family (eight effect-shape families; SV enrichment in scale or probability; frequency and LD dependence; non-additivity), with sealed seeds.

5The All of Us study, set up and not yet run

About 100,000 multi-ancestry All of Us participants with imputed genomes, from a long-read-called reference panel.

snv_indelevery record under 50 bp
with_svevery record, plus GATK-SV short-read calls as their own rows
  • Paired arms, fitted separately on the same people, folds, covariates and seeds.
  • Five kinship-grouped outer folds (KING > 2−3.5 kept together). Per fold: fit on about 72%, calibrate on 8%, evaluate on 20%.
  • Endpoints: log loss and Brier score of frozen, calibrated probabilities; AUC; calibration intercept and slope. A paired one-sided test on one frozen outer fold.
  • Diseases are fitted on a Gaussian working likelihood of 0/1 status, for cost: a coefficient is a per-allele change in probability, not a log odds ratio.
