Figure 2 | SV-PGS is more accurate than LDpred2-auto across simulated genetic architectures. Paired difference in test AUC on the same 10,000 held-out people between SV-PGS (every SNV, indel and imputed SV) and LDpred2-auto (HapMap3-density SNVs), both trained on the same 40,000 people: replicates (grey), mean and Student-t 95% interval (blue; an arrowhead marks one that runs past 0.11), n replicates at right. SV-PGS had the higher AUC in 57 of 57 replicates. The baseline architecture has liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants and 20% causal SVs with SV effects drawn like small-variant effects, and every other changes one thing of it: '3×' triples the SV effect SD, 'a few very large' draws SV effects from a Student t with 3 degrees of freedom, 'all one size' gives every causal SV the same effect size with a random sign, 'well-imputed' draws causal SVs only from those imputed with r² ≥ 0.8, and 'common' only from SVs of frequency ≥ 5%. Not yet replicated three times at 40,000: a few very large SV effects; only well-imputed SVs; only well-imputed SVs, 3× effect; only common SVs; prevalence 1%.
