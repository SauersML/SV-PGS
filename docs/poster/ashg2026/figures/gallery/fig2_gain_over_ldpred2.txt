Figure 2 | SV-PGS is more accurate than LDpred2-auto across simulated genetic architectures. Paired difference in test AUC on the same 10,000 held-out people between SV-PGS (every SNV, indel and imputed SV) and LDpred2-auto (HapMap3-density SNVs), both trained on the same 40,000 people: replicates (grey), mean and Student-t 95% interval (blue); n replicates at right. SV-PGS had the higher AUC in 57 of 57 replicates. Unless named, an architecture has liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants and 20% causal SVs, with SV effects drawn from the same distribution as small-variant effects; '3× SV effect' triples the SV effect SD, 'well-imputed' draws causal SVs only from those Beagle imputes with r² ≥ 0.8 and 'common' only from SVs of frequency ≥ 5%. Not yet replicated three times at 40,000: heavy-tailed sv effects, only well-imputed svs, only well-imputed svs, 3× effect, only common svs, prevalence 1%.
