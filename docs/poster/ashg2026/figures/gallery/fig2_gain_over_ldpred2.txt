Figure 2 | SV-PGS is more accurate than LDpred2-auto across simulated genetic architectures. Paired difference in test AUC (same 10,000 held-out people) between SV-PGS with SVs and LDpred2-auto, per replicate (small dots), with the mean and its Student-t 95% interval, at the largest training size with at least three replicates (N, right). Wins: replicates in which SV-PGS had the higher AUC (74 of 77 overall). Unless named, each architecture has liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants and 20% causal SVs; '3× effect' triples the SV effect variance, 'well-tagged' draws causal SVs only from those imputed with r² ≥ 0.8, 'common' only from SVs of frequency ≥ 5%.
