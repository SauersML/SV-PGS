Figure 3 | SVs add accuracy when they are measured well; imputation removes most of the gain. Paired difference in test AUC between SV-PGS fitted with and without SVs, when every SV is measured by its true genotype (dark) or imputed by Beagle from SNV haplotypes (light), on one 3 Mb chromosome 22 window (60,000 records). Means with Student-t 95% intervals over replicates, training sizes 5,000-40,000 pooled. The true-genotype interval excludes zero for 7 of 17 architectures. Same simulated people, truth and fits; only the SV genotypes differ.
