Figure 3 | SVs add accuracy when they are measured well; imputation removes most of the gain. Paired difference in test AUC on the same held-out people between SV-PGS fitted with and without SVs, when every SV is measured by its true genotype (dark) or imputed by Beagle from SNV haplotypes (light), on one 3 Mb chromosome 22 window (60,000 records). Means with Student-t 95% intervals over replicates, training sizes 5,000-40,000 pooled; n replicates at right (true, imputed). The true-genotype interval lies above zero for 7 of 17 architectures. Same simulated people, truth and fits; only the SV genotypes differ. Unless named, an architecture has liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants and 20% causal SVs, with SV effects drawn from the same distribution as small-variant effects; '3× SV effect' triples the SV effect SD, 'well-imputed' draws causal SVs only from those Beagle imputes with r² ≥ 0.8 and 'common' only from SVs of frequency ≥ 5%.
