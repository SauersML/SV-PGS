Figure 4 | The gain from adding SVs by architecture and training size. Mean paired difference in test AUC on the same held-out people between SV-PGS fitted with and without SVs on one chromosome 22 window, with SVs measured by their true genotypes (a) or imputed (b). Bold on full colour: the Student-t 95% interval over replicates excludes zero ('<0.001': a gain below 0.001 whose interval excludes zero); grey on pale colour: it does not. Colour saturates at ±0.013. Dashes: fewer than two replicates. 40,000-person window fits are not yet replicated and are left out. Unless named, an architecture has liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants and 20% causal SVs, with SV effects drawn from the same distribution as small-variant effects; '3× SV effect' triples the SV effect SD, 'well-imputed' draws causal SVs only from those Beagle imputes with r² ≥ 0.8 and 'common' only from SVs of frequency ≥ 5%.
