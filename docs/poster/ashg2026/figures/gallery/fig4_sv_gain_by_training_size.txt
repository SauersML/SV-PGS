Figure 4 | The gain from adding SVs at each training size from 5,000 to 20,000. Mean paired difference in test AUC between SV-PGS fitted with and without SVs on one chromosome 22 window, with SVs measured by their true genotypes (filled) or imputed (open), and its Student-t 95% interval; arrowheads mark intervals that run past −0.03 or 0.05. A cell is drawn with at least three replicates (n < 3 marks one with fewer). The 40,000-person window fits number 0-3 per architecture and genotype source, too few to show apart; Figure 3 pools them with the other sizes. The baseline architecture has liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants and 20% causal SVs with SV effects drawn like small-variant effects, and every other changes one thing of it: '3×' triples the SV effect SD, 'a few very large' draws SV effects from a Student t with 3 degrees of freedom, 'all one size' gives every causal SV the same effect size with a random sign, 'well-imputed' draws causal SVs only from those imputed with r² ≥ 0.8, and 'common' only from SVs of frequency ≥ 5%.
