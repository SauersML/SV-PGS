Figure 1 | SV-PGS gains accuracy faster than LDpred2-auto as the training sample grows, by the same margin whether 0%, 5% or 20% of the causal variants are SVs. A simulated binary trait on chromosome 22 (liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants, SV effects drawn like small-variant effects), scored in the same 10,000 held-out people by SV-PGS fitted on every SNV, indel and imputed SV and by LDpred2-auto fitted on HapMap3-density SNVs of the same training people. a, Test AUC, mean and Student-t 95% interval over the replicates of all three settings pooled (42, 37, 38, 16 replicates at 5k, 10k, 20k and 40k). Dotted: the AUC of a score that captures all of h² (Wray et al. 2010), the most any method can reach. b, The paired difference in AUC between the methods on the same held-out people for each setting separately, mean and Student-t 95% interval over its replicates; the three overlap at every size, which is why a pools them. bench-sim v7 cohort from 1000 Genomes haplotypes, Beagle-imputed SVs; fitted on MSI.
