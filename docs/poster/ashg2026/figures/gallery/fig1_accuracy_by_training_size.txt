Figure 1 | SV-PGS is more accurate than LDpred2-auto at every training size, by a similar margin whether 0%, 5% or 20% of the causal variants are SVs, so the gain does not come from causal SVs. A simulated binary trait on chromosome 22 (liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants) scored in the same 10,000 held-out individuals. SV-PGS fits every SNV, indel and imputed SV, and LDpred2-auto HapMap3-density SNVs of the same training individuals. a, Test AUC, mean and Student-t 95% interval over the three settings' simulations pooled (41, 37, 38, 16 at 5k-40k). Dotted line: the largest AUC any score can reach, that of the true genetic value at h² = 0.1 (Wray et al. 2010). b, Paired difference in AUC on the same held-out individuals, per setting, mean and Student-t 95% interval.
