Figure 1 | SV-PGS gains accuracy faster than LDpred2-auto as the training sample grows, whatever the share of causal SVs. Test AUC in 10,000 held-out people for a simulated binary trait on chromosome 22 (liability h² = 0.1, prevalence 10%, 200 causal variants) in which 0%, 5% or 20% of the causal variants are SVs with the same per-variant effect distribution as small variants. SV-PGS fits every SNV, indel and imputed SV; LDpred2-auto fits HapMap3-density SNVs of the same training people. Points are means over simulation replicates (5-15 per point) with Student-t 95% intervals, offset horizontally for legibility; the dashed line is the expected AUC of a score capturing all of h² (Daetwyler et al. 2008; Wray et al. 2010). bench-sim v7 cohort from 1000 Genomes haplotypes, Beagle-imputed SVs; fitted on MSI.
