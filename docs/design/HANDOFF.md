The speed gap to mr.ash is 21× (129 s against 6 s); its profile is below. The inference decision (definition of done, item 3) waits on the 20-gene loso/AFR run (`svpgs_mean_field` through the harness, within-group partial r² beside `mr_ashr_init`) and the EP arm of the same gene on the merged engine. The mean-field inference itself costs 26 CPU-s of the 1,825 (124 sweeps 13.5 s, 29 linear responses 8.4 s); **the hyper step is 94% (3 calls, 1,715 s)**: each certified-V evaluation is `_standardized` (the Tierney–Kadane cumulants of D ≈ 314 directions, 0.66 s) plus ~18 replaced line integrals at 0.21 s each (QAGI on the folded range, 60–120 kernel evaluations of p × K per line, the direction's scale part moving every kernel row), and the search makes 60–150 such evaluations per step (three starts at every stage, edges, the stationarity slopes' four extra evaluations per interior weight). That is the speed gap to the definition of done (item 5: CPU per gene at most numba mr.ash's): the floor for a weights search on a handful of penalty weights is tens of evidence evaluations, and each evaluation's floor is one p × K pass plus the integrals of the few directions whose Tierney–Kadane term exceeds their share (the terms: maximum ~10³, median 10⁻¹³ on this gene). The levers, in order: a cheap bound that skips the cumulants of directions that cannot exceed their share; fewer evaluations in the search; a quadrature that spends its points where the integrand is not Gaussian.


**The λ = ∞ start and the trust radius (`ccfb72c`) [real, gene 1].** The weights start at every block's λ = ∞ edge (the log-normal null space of the D3 penalty, per class), where V has no corrections, and `_maximize_evidence` releases a block inward only on a resolved negative Laplace slope at the upper end of its resolvable range. Two outer-loop faults surfaced on the way and are fixed: (1) `_proposal` returned Newton's full step where B + S was positive definite; on the all-edge start's 2-dimensional model (curvature 3e-5 against a gradient of 0.02) that step was 613 units long and could not be refused, because at the trial the density had collapsed onto one lattice node and every derivative was at rounding, so the vanished gradient read as a maximum (`NoCertifiedProgress` on the mean-field test problem; the loop then random-walked on a rounding-level spectrum). The step is now More–Sorensen's maximizer inside the trust radius in both cases (the Cauchy radius first, doubled when a step reaches it, halved on a refusal). (2) A polished state whose whole joint trial re-measured the model's remainder below the one its plan carried (0.106 nats from the first whole trial out of the uncertified start, 1e-6 at the polished state's zero-length trial) was returned uncertified with the stale value; it now replans, at most once per measured decrease. With both, gene 1 certifies in 129 s (SNV) and 141 s (SNV + SV): 3 hyper steps of ~10 s, 22 outer states, 48 mean-field fixed points (13 s), 17 outer iterations and 29 halvings.

**The interior, measured at its own fixed point [sim-only, the mean-field test problem; `b6797a1`].** With the release at the range's centre (`23e0785`), the hyper step's model finds the class-0 roughness block's interior 2.0–2.8 nats above the edge (V 85.09 at ρ = 5.3 against the edge's 83.07, both at the edge's fixed point with the anchored local model). Taken as a trial and polished at its own fixed point, that interior certifies at 80.83 ± 0.005: the local model, 43 units from its anchor, overstates it by 4 nats, and the edge stands. The outer loop now takes a release as a provisional trial (its own fixed point, no gain test), polishes x there, and keeps it only where the polished certified V beats the state it left by more than the tolerance and both errors; otherwise it returns and holds that block at its edge in later hyper steps (`hyper_step(held_edges)`), so the certificate at the edge closes with the interior measured and refuted rather than unsearched. On gene 1 [real] no centre trial passes the Laplace screen (the interior is level or far below), and the fit is unchanged.

**What the hyper step did on gene 1 from the unit-weight start [real; traced with `lead/gene1_trace_v3.py` on main `d5b8c01`].** The certified prior it returned was the same λ = ∞ edge of every block (after three steps of 736 s, 807 s and 14 s the weights were [∞, ∞, ∞, ∞, 0.001]; the edge is cheap, no corrections). The interior was never searched: at the start weights the Laplace V's ρ-gradient is 0.04–0.17 (its trust-region ascent stops at once, ½|g|² under the tolerance), while the corrections' slopes are +1.7, −22.7, +22.3 and +0.07 with a-posteriori errors 0.7–1.8 against targets of 0.19, so the stationarity gain is 4.8 nats and every full-gradient Newton trial is refused. The Tierney–Kadane terms along ~20 of the 314 standardized directions are 10²–10³ (the median direction's is 10⁻¹³): those are near-collapse directions, and the corrected V is a sum of their exact line integrals along eigen-directions that rotate with ρ, so its central differences in ρ see the replaced set's corrections move between blocks (the antisymmetric ±22 pair). The interior V of −0.69 at the unmoved weights lost to the edge's 0.0 by that much. So on real data the engine's certified evidence works only at the edges, and the interior fit (a rougher, data-shaped mixing density) is out of reach until the corrections are continuous in ρ: either integrated over the replaced directions' span as one subspace (not per line), or the collapse directions treated by the ruling's other half (the width → 0 limit as the Gaussian-prior boundary model). The λ = ∞ start does not change this: its inward-release test asks for a resolved Laplace slope at the range's upper end, where the slope is O(e^{−ρ}) by construction, so the interior is still unsearched (a per-block scan of the certified V across the resolvable range on gene 1 is queued, `lead/gene1_rho_scan.py`, to measure whether the interior holds anything on real data). This, not the inference, is what stands between SV-PGS and the accuracy target.
The 20-gene pooled runs of the run branch never produced a fit record (the outer loop's non-terminating cycle and its uncertifiable certificate, both fixed on main at `eef5f00`: an unresolved inner step is never accepted, and the certificate's resolvable pieces are tightened per decision). ## Process
