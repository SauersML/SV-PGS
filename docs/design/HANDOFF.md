**"Certified"** means a fit met its numerical stopping criterion on the computed objective: remaining gain at most 1/(2K) nats on V, the Laplace evidence with the leading Tierney–Kadane term along each standardized direction. That term has no remainder bound, so a certified V is not within 1/(2K) of the exact evidence (CAPABILITIES.md). The artifact records it as `outer_criterion_met`; `model_acceptance` quarantines a model without it.

## On main
- **Workspace pipeline** (`sv_pgs/workspace_pipeline.py`): samples, store, phenotypes, cohort, measurement, direct_sv, fit, score, effects, report, export. Each step is keyed by its inputs, upstream keys and code; a finished step is never redone. Inputs on a mounted bucket are keyed by object generation (`content_identity`).
- **Batch launcher** (`launcher/workspace/`): each job runs on a new disk, so the run lives in the workspace bucket (`sv_pgs/run_mirror.py`: restored before the driver, pushed every 15 minutes and at exit; superseded store copies are linked to the mount, not downloaded). The budget guard reserves each slot once.
- **Fit** (`stage2_wiring.fit_models`): models sharing training rows, covariates and likelihood are fitted together on one Stage 0. Stage 2's reads are sharded by LD block over every GPU of the budget. The mean-field fixed point runs on the samples or, where a pass measures cheaper, on Stage 0's LD band with the exact far field (a round that lowers the exact ELBO is redone under a proximal minorizer). A band-route model that meets its stopping criterion continues on the sample route before acceptance; unresolved models are refitted individually on that route.
- **Acceptance:** a quarantined model is never scored or reported. Its trait leaves every table and is listed in `report/quarantined.tsv`; the other traits are reported.

## Open engine work
- **Certification at the width → 0 boundary.** On some real genes the evidence has no interior maximum along the mixing density's width direction (B + S negative there to the boundary, a ridge prior in the limit), and the fit returns uncertified there. How often this happens at biobank scale is unmeasured; the pilot measures it. The pinned case, `tests/test_mean_field.py::test_the_mean_field_fit_certifies` (strict xfail), measured 2026-10-14: the first empirical Bayes ends uncertified with one class's density a spike (0.84 of its mass on the lattice's bottom node, below the kernel floor) and a state error twice the tolerance; the lattice check then refines and extends the lattice (the moments move 1.5 and 1.0 nats), and the continuation there finds the weights' difference curvature indefinite and walks x outside every basin, so the fit keeps the first solve. The plan log now gives the weights' gain by its parts (decrement, fold, exact-slope floor) or names a release, which separates these cases in a run's log.
- **The walk outside every basin (fixed 2026-10-14).** An annotation smooth's penalty null space (its linear term, a missing state, a group the penalty leaves whole) had a flat prior. For a group of signal-free variants the evidence rises without bound as the group's scale falls, so the empirical Bayes walked that coefficient to |x| ~ 5e4 and its maximizations to 1e15 (`tests/test_bench_sim_derived.py`: 50 s or more than 10 minutes by route). Each group's null space now has its own learned ridge (the double penalty, `scale_mixture_prior`), started at its λ = ∞ edge like every block: that test takes 8 s and 19 s on the two routes, and bench-sim's annotated synthetic fit, quarantined uncertified before, certifies.
- **A value comparable across fixed points.** `settle_release` and `_path_gain` compare V on one continuous branch of fixed points, not across two (V omits the cavity-dependent Gaussian part of the evidence).
- **The certificate's remaining terms:** the fixed-point term and the C-variation slope (theory-ep), and the normal-means cases pinned as strict expected failures in `tests/test_scale_mixture_ep.py`.
- **Not wired, by design so far:** the external-GWAS annotations (`external_annotations`), the SV-context prior features (`sv_prior_features`), and the full-data EP route (`inference="ep"`, bench-sim's `svpgs_ep` submission), each waiting on a measured held-out gain.
