Since 2026-09-21: the weights start at every block's λ = ∞ edge and a block is released at the centre of its resolvable range where the certified V beats the edge (the Laplace form screens the trial); a release is taken as a provisional trial at its own fixed point, polished there, and kept only where the polished certified V beats the state it left (a refused block is held at its edge by the model's later hyper steps); every Newton step of the outer loop and of the coefficient maximizer is bounded by a trust radius that starts at the Cauchy length; the objective carries its own rounding bound (the verification harness's), a step the value cannot resolve is judged on the gradient, and a curvature that is not resolvably positive definite certifies nothing; the mean-field fixed point's cavity response carries the noise's own response (B is the profile's curvature); the kernel rows and the exact repeats are held across the outer loop's iterations.
