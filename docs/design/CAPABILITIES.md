# What the public routes do today

[SPEC.md](../../SPEC.md) states the model this project is committed to and [MODEL.md](MODEL.md) derives
it. Both are requirements. This page is the description: one row for each requirement the shipped code does not yet
meet, what it does instead, and the test that pins that behaviour today. Change a row in the commit that changes the
behaviour; delete a row when the code meets the requirement.

The public routes are `sv_pgs.fit_model.fit` (the `sv-pgs fit` command, over a dosage store),
`sv_pgs.small_n.fit_small_n` (the dense small-n route that
`benchmarks/svpgs_method.py` and `benchmarks/svpgs_small_n.py` enter), `sv_pgs.artifact.predict` (the `sv-pgs score`
command) and `sv_pgs.workspace_pipeline.run_pipeline` (the `sv-pgs workspace-run` command).

| The requirement | What the code does today | The test that pins it |
|---|---|---|
| A continuous annotation enters the prior "as a smooth function with learned smoothness"; every variant's prior variance depends on its SV context. | The store route (`stage2_wiring`) and the small-n route (`small_n`) pass the store's sidecar columns through `annotation_design` as smooths (the workspace store's `sv_length`, `in_gene`, `in_exon`, `log_tss_distance`, and the strict input's `n_paths`, `n_paths_total`, `cx`), plus the frequency function. The SV-context features themselves (how strongly a record tags a nearby SV, the local SV landscape) are not computed by any store converter yet (WORKSPACE_PIPELINE.md, open item 4). | `tests/test_annotation_design.py` |
| Prior hyperparameters are "pooled hierarchically across classes and traits". | Classes share one prior in every route. Traits and folds do not: `stage2_wiring.fit_models` groups models with the same rows, covariates and likelihood to share genotype reads, while fitting each model's hyperparameters and acceptance separately. The cross-gene pooled fit (`pooled_fit`) was deleted 2026-09-22. | none: no route pools |
| "A logistic likelihood for diseases" (MODEL.md §1). | The workspace run fits every disease on a Gaussian working likelihood of its 0/1 status (`workspace_pipeline.FIT_TRAIT_TYPE`; the config's `disease_likelihood`, `gaussian_working` in the template), so a coefficient is a per-allele change in the probability of recorded status, not a log odds ratio, and a fitted value is not a probability. The logistic likelihood exists as its Polya-Gamma bound (`binary_likelihood`, through `stage2_wiring` and `small_n`, selected by a binary trait type in `fit_model.fit`, or by `disease_likelihood: "bernoulli"` in the workspace), a variational lower bound rather than EP's tilted moments. Its metric moves with its sites, so every sweep re-reads the store; the template does not select it, for that cost. | `tests/test_workspace_pipeline.py` (the fit calls' trait types), `tests/test_binary_streamed.py`, `tests/test_binary_small_n.py` |
| "Any grid used to compute with it is only a quadrature rule. It is refined until results change by less than a derived tolerance." | The store route starts from the single-variant likelihoods at the covariate-only noise (`stage0_lattice`), then checks the fitted density on refined and extended lattices and continues affected fits there. An unresolved quadrature check quarantines that model. These comparisons are numerical checks on the fitted approximation, not a remainder bound for the exact evidence; an accepted completion on the large chr22 recovery case is still pending. | `tests/test_scale_mixture_ep.py`, `tests/test_stage2_wiring.py` |
| "The inference is empirical Bayes: hyperparameters are type-II maximum likelihood under an expectation-propagation approximation." | Two inferences exist. `fit_small_n(inference=...)` defaults to EP; the store route (`stage2_wiring.fit_models`, which `sv-pgs fit` and the workspace run use) defaults to mean-field coordinate ascent (`full_data_fit._FullDataMeanField`), with EP behind `inference="ep"`. The bench-real gene runs use mean field. Which one survives is the open measurement in HANDOFF.md. | `tests/test_small_n.py::test_the_small_n_fit_certifies_and_scores`, `tests/test_mean_field.py::test_the_mean_field_fit_scores` |
| "A fit is accepted only with its convergence certificate." | A fit is returned and saved with its certificate terms whether or not its criteria were met. The artifact carries a typed acceptance decision (`model_acceptance.acceptance`): a model whose returned state failed a check (`outer_criterion_met` false, an unresolved or restored state, an exhausted budget, an unresolved quadrature or a nonzero summary-only far-field scale) is uncertified, and in the workspace it is still scored and in every table, listed with its codes in `uncertified.tsv`. Only a model with no score (non-finite parameters, or a failed group fit returned as its covariates alone) is refused (`model_acceptance.require_scorable`, `artifact.predict_models`) and listed in `quarantined.tsv`. `outer_criterion_met` records only the outer loop's stopping criterion on the computed objective: the outer steps charge the fixed points' own error as zero (`OuterFit.fixed_point_term_measured` is False), so no fit is certified in the sense MODEL.md §4 states. The mean-field interior state does not meet the criterion (HANDOFF.md Next 0/0a). | `tests/test_fit_model.py::test_the_artifact_reports_the_outer_criterion_and_never_certification`, `tests/test_readiness_regressions.py::test_acceptance_quarantines_unresolved_fits_but_allows_null_notes`, `tests/test_readiness_regressions.py::test_an_uncertified_model_is_scorable_but_a_failed_fit_is_not`, `tests/test_mean_field.py::test_the_mean_field_fit_certifies` (strict xfail) |
| The evidence V is "certified to tol" (MODEL.md §4). | V is the Laplace evidence plus, along each standardized eigen-direction of −H, the leading Tierney–Kadane term κ4/8 + 5κ3²/24; the directions with the largest terms are replaced by one-dimensional quadratures until the rest sum to at most tol/2 (`scale_mixture_ep._laplace_corrections_once`). The tolerance holds for the quadratures and the retained leading terms, not for V's error: the terms have no remainder bound (log f = −x²/2 − x⁶ has zero third and fourth cumulants and a true correction of about −0.448), and mixed cumulants between directions are left out (log f = −x²/2 − y² − x²y² has Gaussian axis slices and a joint correction of about −0.236). Every comparison of V, and every stopping test on it, is a numerical convergence criterion on this computed objective. | none |
| "K exact posterior draws by perturb-and-solve" (MODEL.md §4). | Only the EP route's draws are perturb-and-solve draws, from its Gaussian approximation q. The mean-field routes (the store route's default and small-n's) draw from the fitted product approximation at fixed hyperparameters (`draw_laws.ProductMixtureDraws`): along a direction that mixes variants in LD its variance is neither an upper nor a lower bound on the posterior's, and hyperparameter uncertainty is left out (`fast_scoring`). Every interval, P(effect > 0) and effect SD is a statement about that law. | `tests/test_draw_laws.py` |
| "A variant is left out of computation only when a derived bound shows its posterior contribution to every score is below the certificate tolerance" (SPEC). | The 8-bit code `(DS_milli·127 + 500) // 1000` stores DS 0.000–0.003 as 0, so a record whose only nonzero dosages are that small is stored monomorphic and no model sees it. No bound is computed for it; the store step counts these records (`encoding_flattened` in the store summary). | `tests/test_store_converter.py::test_records_the_8_bit_codes_make_monomorphic_are_counted_across_batches` |
| "A stored dosage is a measurement of the true genotype, with a truth-calibrated reliability r². GATK-SV short-read calls are a second measurement ... and the two are fused." | r² reaches the fit as the prior's log-variance offset, from the caller or from the store's `quality` column (`stage2_wiring.store_log_reliability`). `measurement_model` and `sv_fusion` are imported by no fitting route; they build stores, the workspace's measurement step and its `direct_sv` step. That step adds each kept GATK-SV record as a store row of its own beside the imputed records (`gatksv_store_rows`), with the measurement model's no-truth offset from its reported r² (1 for a hard-call ALT count, the CNQ bound for a copy number, −inf for a row with neither). The two measurements are not fused: `fit_model.fit` takes no leakage map, so a paired GATK-SV row and imputed record stay two columns, and `sv_fusion`'s pairs serve only the no-call fill and the grouping. It has run on synthetic inputs only. bench-real passes no offsets at all. | `tests/test_fit_model.py::test_the_driver_gets_the_records_log_reliabilities`, `tests/test_workspace_pipeline.py::test_a_gatksv_call_set_adds_direct_rows_that_only_with_sv_fits` |
| `sv-pgs workspace-run` fits the workspace's cohort. | It runs every step on synthetic inputs with the production measurement model, fitter and scorer; it has not run on All of Us data. | `tests/test_workspace_pipeline.py` (stand-ins for the fitter) |
| "With several GPUs, all visible CUDA devices share the resident genotype work" (SPEC). | Stage 0 does: one buffer per device, whole chromosomes each (`genotype_statistics.run_genotype_pass`). Stage 2's reads do too wherever the budget has more than one CUDA device: `stage2_wiring.stage2_source` shards them by LD block (`device_plan.ShardedDualSource`: each block's products on its own device, a read's images summed on the first), on a model's training samples. The state a fit keeps between reads (the dual solver's sample-side arrays, the mean-field oracle's moments, the band) lives on the first device, and the device sweep decodes a peer device's tile rather than run its code-panel kernel on it. Validated on the host only (numpy standing in for CuPy); the two-device CUDA tests need two GPUs. A device that is exposed but unusable is an error (`compute_budget.check_visible_devices`). | `tests/test_device_plan.py` (the plan, the exposed-device check, and the sharded source against the streamed one over two host "devices"); `tests/test_device_plan_cuda.py` (two GPUs) |
